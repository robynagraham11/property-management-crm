When to use: once per 12 months, at least 3 full months before the effective date, at or under the provincial cap.

How to open and share this portal — 3 ways

  1. Easiest (recommended): in the CRM folder, double-click Start CRM.command, then visit http://localhost:3000/portal.html — everything opens in your browser with working links.
  2. Offline: double-click public/portal.html. Everything works; the .md documents may download instead of previewing — open them in Word, Google Docs, or any text editor.
  3. Share it: copy the public folder (the portal plus the toolkit folder inside it) to any web host or cloud drive, or email the folder. Keep the two together — the portal links to the toolkit folder.

Tip: press Ctrl/Cmd + P to print the portal as a paper reference.

Scope: forms, disclosures, and general BC residential tenancy guidance only. Commercial properties and strata-corporation management are out of scope.

Not legal advice: this toolkit was drafted with AI assistance. It does not replace review by a BC lawyer or your managing broker, and official forms, fees, and caps change — always download the current version from the official source and verify current amounts (rent-increase cap, deposit limits, interest, RTB fees) before use.
