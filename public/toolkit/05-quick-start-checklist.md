## 0. Before any client work (compliance gate)

- [ ] BCFSA rental property management licence active; engaged with Stonehaus Realty Corp. and managing broker
- [ ] Business licence from City of Langley and/or Township of Langley (confirm which)
- [ ] Trust account setup approved by brokerage (or pooled account access)
- [ ] Brokerage E&O insurance confirmed; brokerage policy manual read
- [ ] Download the current official forms: RTB-1, RTB-7, RTB-27, RTB-30, RTB-32, RTB-33, DORT, DROUP (rental PM), Form K
- [ ] Record-keeping system ready (7-year retention; trust ledgers per property)

## 1. New owner onboarding

- [ ] Give and have owner sign **DORT** (before providing services)
- [ ] Sign **Property Management Agreement** (Model A/B/C) or **Tenant Placement Service Agreement**
- [ ] Collect: proof of ownership, property insurance certificate, keys, emergency contacts
- [ ] Strata unit? Obtain **Form B**, bylaws, rules; check rental restrictions; prepare **Form K** for tenants
- [ ] Verify the suite is lawful (municipal; strata) and document condition before marketing

## 2. New tenancy (placement)

- [ ] Market at approved rent; run consistent, lawful showings
- [ ] Rental Application with **PIPA consent** signed before screening
- [ ] Screen consistently: ID, income, references, credit (no discrimination)
- [ ] Give unrepresented applicant **DROUP (rental PM)** per brokerage policy
- [ ] Sign **RTB-1** + addenda (Pet, Strata if applicable); give tenant a copy within **21 days**; provide landlord's name/address (RTA s. 12)
- [ ] Collect first month's rent + deposits **within RTA limits** into the **trust account**; issue receipts
- [ ] Complete **RTB-27 move-in inspection** jointly; give tenant a copy
- [ ] Strata unit? Tenant signs **Form K**; give bylaws/rules copy
- [ ] Optional: PAD authorization for rent
- [ ] Hand over keys; record move-in date in the CRM

## 3. During the tenancy

- [ ] Collect and receipt rent on time; if overdue, serve **RTB-30** promptly (watch the 5-day rule)
- [ ] Maintenance requests logged; routine vs. emergency spend limits respected
- [ ] Any non-emergency entry: serve **Entry Notice** ≥24h ahead, 8 a.m.–9 p.m.
- [ ] Log all tenant complaints and repairs; follow brokerage record policy
- [ ] Monthly: owner statement + trust reconciliation

## 4. Rent increases and renewals

- [ ] Check the **current provincial cap** on the RTB website (VERIFY each year)
- [ ] Serve **RTB-7** at least **3 full months** before the effective date, and only if **12 months** have passed since the last increase/tenancy start
- [ ] Fixed term ending? It continues month-to-month — send the tenant a written continuation notice or negotiate a renewal

## 5. Ending tenancies

- [ ] Cause: serve **RTB-32** with a full month's notice
- [ ] Owner use / major reno: **RTB-33** — 4 months' notice + **one month's rent compensation** (rules effective July 18, 2024 — VERIFY current)
- [ ] Tenant's notice received? Acknowledge in writing and book the move-out
- [ ] **RTB-27 move-out inspection** jointly; date-stamped photos
- [ ] Deposit settlement within **15 days** (Form D) or file an RTB application
- [ ] Final owner statement; close or renew tenancy records in the CRM

## 6. Ongoing compliance rhythm

- [ ] Monthly: trust reconciliation with brokerage
- [ ] Quarterly: re-download official forms; check RTB/BCFSA updates
- [ ] Annually: licence and business-licence renewals; E&O confirmation; rent-cap update
- [ ] Continuously: keep every document signed, dated, copied to the other party, and stored

> **Remember:** official RTB/BCFSA forms change. Before every use, download the current version. When in doubt, ask your managing broker — and keep a BC lawyer for the agreements.
