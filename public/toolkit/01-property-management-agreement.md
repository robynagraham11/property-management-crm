## 1. Appointment

The Owner appoints the Brokerage, through the Licensee, to provide the **residential rental property management services** selected in section 3 for the Property, on the terms below. This agreement applies to residential tenancies under the *Residential Tenancy Act* (RTA) only; it does not cover commercial premises or strata-corporation management.

## 2. Authority

The Owner authorizes the Manager to, on the Owner's behalf:

- advertise the Property and conduct viewings;
- receive applications and screen applicants (with PIPA consent);
- negotiate and sign a Residential Tenancy Agreement (RTB-1) and lawful addenda;
- collect rent, security deposits and pet damage deposits (within RTA limits) into the brokerage trust account;
- serve and receive notices under the RTA, including rent-increase, entry, and termination notices;
- complete move-in and move-out condition inspection reports (RTB-27);
- coordinate repairs and maintenance within section 7 limits;
- hold keys and access devices, and arrange entry as permitted by the RTA;
- engage licensed tradespersons and service providers in the Owner's name; and
- start or respond to Residential Tenancy Branch (RTB) dispute-resolution proceedings relating to the Property.

The Manager does **not** have authority to sell the Property, mortgage it, or waive the Owner's rights other than as set out in this agreement or in written instructions from the Owner.

## 3. Service model — tick ONE

### ☐ MODEL A — Owner-Managed Support (tenant contact handled by Manager)
The Owner manages rent collection, maintenance and decisions; the Manager acts as the Owner's professional point of contact for tenants:

- receives and handles tenant calls, emails, and messages;
- provides standard RTB-compliant notices and forms for the Owner to serve;
- coordinates move-in/move-out condition inspections (or advises the Owner);
- fields maintenance requests and reports them to the Owner; the Owner approves and pays all work;
- monitors statutory deadlines (rent increases, inspections, notice periods) and reminds the Owner;
- does **not** collect rent, hold trust money, or instruct contractors without specific written instructions.

**Fee:** $____________ per month (or ____________ % of monthly rent), payable monthly in advance. Placements, if requested, are governed by a separate Tenant Placement Service Agreement.

### ☐ MODEL B — Full-Service Management (recommended for hands-off owners)
The Manager handles the full tenancy lifecycle:

- market, advertise, show, and screen applicants;
- execute the tenancy agreement and all addenda, collect deposits;
- collect rent and enforce payment (including serving RTB notices);
- handle maintenance requests, coordinate repairs, and pay vendor invoices from Owner funds;
- conduct move-in/move-out inspections and manage deposit returns;
- serve rent-increase and other notices; manage renewals and move-outs;
- provide monthly statements and year-end summaries.

**Fees:** ____________ % of rent collected each month (market-typical 7–10%), **plus** a one-time leasing fee of ____________ % of the first month's rent for each new tenancy (market-typical 50%). Fees are payable from collected rent; if rent is not collected in a month, no management fee is payable for that month unless the failure is caused by the Manager.

### ☐ MODEL C — Leasing and Tenant Placement Only
The Manager only finds and places a tenant, then steps back:

- market, advertise, show, and screen applicants;
- prepare the tenancy agreement and addenda, collect deposits into the brokerage trust account;
- complete the move-in condition inspection and key handover;
- transfer the tenancy file (agreement, inspection, deposits) to the Owner at handover.

**Fee:** ____________ % of one month's rent (market-typical 75–100%), payable when a tenant signs the tenancy agreement and takes possession. Unless the parties sign a Tenant Placement Service Agreement, no ongoing management is provided.

## 4. Fees, costs, and remuneration

- All fees and remuneration are earned by the **Brokerage** and paid to the Brokerage (RESA s. 35). The Manager is paid by the Brokerage only.
- GST applies to management and leasing fees.
- Out-of-pocket costs (advertising, credit checks, couriers, registration fees, RTB filing fees) are paid by the Owner, on the Manager's invoice, at cost.
- The Owner keeps the Property's bank account funded for maintenance and vendor payments (see section 7).

## 5. Owner's obligations

The Owner will:

- keep valid insurance on the Property (property and liability), name the Brokerage as an **additional insured** if the insurer allows, and give the Manager proof of insurance;
- provide all keys, fobs, manuals, and warranty documents;
- disclose in writing any known defects, hazards, illegal suites, strata bylaws and rental restrictions, and court or RTB proceedings affecting the Property;
- keep sufficient funds available for repairs, strata fees, taxes, and utilities;
- make all decisions the Owner retains (including approving tenants) promptly, and in any event within the time needed to meet RTA deadlines;
- comply with all laws affecting the Property (RTA, RESA, strata bylaws, municipal bylaws, the *Human Rights Code*), including keeping the Property in lawful, habitable condition;
- indemnify the Brokerage and Manager as set out in section 11.

## 6. Manager's obligations

The Manager will act honestly, in good faith, and with the care, skill, and diligence of a reasonably prudent residential property manager, and will:

- comply with RESA, the RTA, BCFSA rules, and brokerage policy;
- hold all trust money in the brokerage trust account and keep proper records;
- account monthly (Model B) with a statement of rents collected, fees, and expenses;
- screen applicants consistently and without discrimination;
- use the current official RTB forms for every tenancy event;
- keep all Owner funds and tenant deposits separate from brokerage funds;
- maintain confidentiality of Owner and tenant personal information under PIPA;
- disclose to the Owner any conflict of interest promptly.

## 7. Maintenance and repairs

- **Routine:** the Manager may authorize individual repairs up to **$____________** (suggested $300) without prior approval.
- **Above that amount:** the Manager will obtain at least one quote and the Owner's written approval before proceeding.
- **Emergencies:** the Manager may act immediately to protect life, safety, or the Property, and will notify the Owner as soon as reasonably possible.
- The Owner will reimburse the Manager for approved expenses from Owner funds or within 7 days of invoice.

## 8. Money, trust funds, and deposits

- All rents, security deposits, pet damage deposits, and other tenant money received are **trust funds** and will be deposited into the brokerage trust account (RESA s. 25) and administered under BCFSA rules.
- Deposits are collected within RTA limits (currently half a month's rent each for security and pet damage; combined max one month's rent — **verify current limits**) and held in trust until the tenancy ends.
- The Manager will return deposits per RTA s. 21 within 15 days of move-out, deducting only lawful amounts, or file a timely RTB application to retain disputed amounts.
- The Manager will disburse net rents to the Owner monthly by the ________ day of the month (or as agreed in Schedule C).
- Deposit interest (currently 0% — **verify current prescribed rate**) is credited per the RTA.

## 9. Term and termination

- **By the Owner or the Manager:** either party may end this agreement on **30 days' written notice** (or a different period agreed in writing).
- **By the Brokerage/managing broker:** may end or reassign this agreement as required by RESA or brokerage policy on written notice.
- On termination, the Manager will hand over the tenancy file, keys, trust records, and a final statement within a reasonable time (generally 10 business days), and will transfer net trust funds to the Owner after paying fees earned and expenses incurred to that date.
- Termination does not affect the Owner's obligation to pay fees earned before termination, or a leasing fee earned on a tenancy signed before termination.
- If the Owner cancels after approving an applicant but before signing, the Owner will reimburse the Manager's documented out-of-pocket costs.

## 10. Compliance with laws

This agreement is governed by the laws of **British Columbia**, including the *Real Estate Services Act*, the *Residential Tenancy Act*, the *Strata Property Act* (if applicable), and the *Personal Information Protection Act*. If any term conflicts with a mandatory law or with brokerage policy, that term is read down to comply, and the rest of the agreement continues.

## 11. Liability and indemnity

- The Manager is not liable for: tenant conduct or default; the Owner's failure to fund repairs or act on recommendations; damage by fire, flood, or other perils; hidden defects; or the acts of independent tradespersons the Owner approves.
- The Manager's total liability to the Owner under this agreement is limited to the total fees paid by the Owner in the 12 months before the claim (negotiable — review with counsel).
- The Owner indemnifies the Brokerage, Licensee, and managing broker against claims, costs, and RTB orders arising from the Property or the tenancy, except to the extent caused by the Manager's negligence or willful misconduct.
- Nothing in this agreement limits rights a tenant has under the RTA.

## 12. Insurance

The Brokerage maintains errors and omissions insurance as required by RESA. The Owner is responsible for property, liability, and, where applicable, strata-required insurance on the Property. Insurance certificates must be exchanged on request.

## 13. Notices

Notices under this agreement are served in writing by email to the addresses on page 1 (with read confirmation where possible) or by hand/mail to the addresses on page 1. Service by email is deemed received the next business day.

## 14. Dispute resolution

- Disputes between Owner and Manager: first, a good-faith meeting within 14 days of written notice of dispute; then mediation by a mutually agreed mediator; then, if unresolved, arbitration under the BC *Arbitration Act* in Vancouver, BC (single arbitrator), or the BC courts if both parties agree in writing.
- Disputes with tenants are resolved under the RTA through the RTB — not under this section.

## 15. General

- Entire agreement: this agreement (with Schedules) is the whole agreement; changes must be in writing and signed by both parties.
- No assignment by the Owner without the Brokerage's written consent.
- Survival: sections 8, 9, 11, 14 survive termination.
