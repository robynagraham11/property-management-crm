## 1. RESA agency disclosures (BCFSA) — before touching any client file

### 1.1 Disclosure of Representation in Trading Services (DORT) — OFFICIAL
- **What:** discloses that the brokerage represents the **owner**, the agency relationship, and remuneration.
- **When required:** before providing services to an owner — every management or placement engagement.
- **Who signs:** the owner (the brokerage keeps a copy).
- **Where:** BCFSA practice resources (search "Disclosure of Representation in Trading Services"). Use the official form verbatim.

### 1.2 Disclosure of Risks to Unrepresented Parties (DROUP) — Rental Property Management — OFFICIAL
- **What:** warns an **unrepresented tenant** that the brokerage does not represent them.
- **When required:** when you deal with a tenant who has no representation (almost every residential tenancy). Follow your managing broker's policy on timing (typically at application or signing).
- **Who signs:** the tenant.
- **Where:** BCFSA practice resources (the rental-property-management version, not the sales version).

### 1.3 Conflict / dual-representation disclosure — check brokerage policy
- If the brokerage is ever asked to act for both owner and tenant, that requires specific written consent and managing-broker approval. Default rule: the brokerage represents the **owner only**.

## 2. Tenancy documents — landlord/tenant side (RTB official forms)

### 2.1 Residential Tenancy Agreement — RTB-1 — OFFICIAL
- **When required:** for **every** residential tenancy. The RTA requires terms in writing; RTB-1 is the standard form.
- **Who signs:** landlord (the Manager signs as agent only if authorized — see Schedule A of agreement 02) **and** tenant(s).
- **Rules:** fixed terms automatically continue month-to-month; "must vacate" clauses are void; late fees must be stated; service address must be set out.
- **After signing:** give the tenant a copy within **21 days** (RTA s. 13) and give the tenant the landlord's name and address in writing (RTA s. 12 — Manager's address can serve as the address for service).

### 2.2 Addenda to RTB-1 — DRAFTED (in `04`)
- Pet Addendum, Strata Rental Addendum, and any lawful house rules. Attach at signing; each party initials each page.
- **Who signs:** landlord and tenant.

### 2.3 Condition Inspection Report — RTB-27 — OFFICIAL
- **When required:** at **move-in** and at **move-out** (RTA ss. 23–24). Do it jointly with the tenant; the landlord must give the tenant a copy.
- **Who signs:** landlord (or Manager) and tenant.
- **Why:** without a signed move-in report, the landlord generally cannot claim against the security deposit for damage (RTA s. 24).
- **Tip:** date-stamped photos attached to the report are your best evidence.

### 2.4 Notice of Rent Increase — RTB-7 — OFFICIAL
- **When:** once per **12 months**, served **at least 3 full months before** the effective date, at or below the annual provincial cap (3.0% for 2025; 3.0% announced for 2026 — **VERIFY the current cap before each use**).
- **Who signs:** landlord (Manager as agent) only; serve per RTA service rules.
- **Email service:** only if the tenant has **agreed in writing** to email service (check current RTB guidance/form).

### 2.5 10-Day Notice to End Tenancy for Unpaid Rent or Utilities — RTB-30 — OFFICIAL
- **When:** rent or utility payment overdue.
- **Effect:** tenant has **5 days** to pay and cancel; if served **3 or more times in 12 months**, the tenant may lose the right to cancel (VERIFY current rules).
- **Who signs:** landlord (Manager as agent); served on tenant.

### 2.6 One-Month Notice to End Tenancy for Cause — RTB-32 — OFFICIAL
- **When:** breach of a material term (repeated late payment, damage, unreasonable disturbance, illegal acts, unauthorized occupants, etc.).
- **Who signs:** landlord (Manager as agent); served on tenant. Date must give a full month.

### 2.7 Four-Month Notice to End Tenancy for Landlord's Use — RTB-33 — OFFICIAL
- **When:** the owner (or close family, or a purchaser) genuinely intends to live in the unit; or major renovation. **4 months' notice** and **one month's rent as compensation** to the tenant (requirements changed July 18, 2024 — **VERIFY current rules**).
- **Limits:** must be in good faith; if the owner doesn't move in within a reasonable time, the tenant can claim **12 months' rent** compensation. Cannot take effect before a fixed term ends.
- **Who signs:** landlord (Manager as agent); served on tenant.

### 2.8 Tenant's One-Month Notice to End Tenancy — OFFICIAL (RTB form for tenants)
- **When:** tenant ends a month-to-month tenancy. Keep a copy on file. (Numbered form — download current from RTB; do not serve it for the tenant, but the tenant must sign it.)

### 2.9 Notice of Landlord's Entry — DRAFTED (in `04`)
- **When:** any non-emergency entry (inspections, showings, repairs). At least **24 hours' written notice**, between **8:00 a.m. and 9:00 p.m.**, stating date, time, and purpose (RTA s. 29). No notice for genuine emergencies or with tenant's consent.
- **Who signs:** landlord (Manager as agent).

### 2.10 Application for Dispute Resolution — OFFICIAL (RTB)
- **When:** any dispute you cannot resolve (unpaid rent, damage, deposit retention, notice disputes). Current filing fee **$100** (VERIFY).
- **Who signs:** the filing party (landlord or tenant). The Manager files for the owner under the PMA authority.

### 2.11 Security and pet damage deposits — rules and DRAFTED receipt (in `04`)
- **Limits:** security deposit max **half a month's rent**; pet damage deposit max **half a month's rent**; combined max **one month's rent** (VERIFY current maximums).
- **Interest:** currently **0%** (VERIFY current prescribed rate).
- **Return:** within **15 days** of move-out, with a written statement of any deductions (RTA s. 21), or file an RTB application. Use the DRAFTED Deposit Receipt & Settlement Statement in `04`.
- **Cash rent:** landlord must give a receipt for cash (RTA s. 26) — DRAFTED receipt in `04`.

## 3. Strata-related disclosures (Strata Property Act) — for strata-titled units

### 3.1 Form B — Information Certificate — OFFICIAL (issued by the strata corporation)
- **What:** confirms strata fees, arrears, and **rental restrictions** bylaws.
- **When:** **before** listing/managing a strata unit; and updated before each new tenancy.
- **Who:** the strata corporation issues it (a fee may apply — VERIFY current Form B fee rules). The Owner obtains it and gives the Manager a copy.

### 3.2 Form K — Notice of Tenant's Responsibilities — OFFICIAL
- **What:** tells the tenant their obligations under the strata bylaws and rules.
- **When required:** give to the tenant **at the start of the tenancy**; the tenant signs acknowledgment (SPA s. 146).
- **Who signs:** the tenant (landlord gives the form).

### 3.3 Bylaws and rules package + Strata Rental Addendum — DRAFTED (in `04`)
- Give the tenant the **current bylaws and rules** with the tenancy agreement (they bind the tenant). Confirm whether the strata has a **rental restriction bylaw** before signing any tenancy (SPA ss. 141–144).

## 4. Privacy (PIPA)

- Collect, use, and keep tenant/owner personal information only for lawful purposes, with consent, and store it securely. The DRAFTED Rental Application in `04` includes the PIPA consent statement.
- Never share screening information with anyone but the owner and the brokerage.
