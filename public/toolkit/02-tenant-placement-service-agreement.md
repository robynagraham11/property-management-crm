## 1. The service — what the Manager will do

During the term, the Manager will use reasonable efforts to find and place a suitable residential tenant for the Property, including:

- recommending and advertising at a market rent (with the Owner's written approval);
- arranging and conducting viewings;
- receiving applications and screening applicants consistently and lawfully, including identity, income/employment, credit, and landlord-reference checks (with the applicant's PIPA consent);
- not discriminating contrary to the BC *Human Rights Code*;
- preparing the Residential Tenancy Agreement (official RTB-1) and lawful addenda;
- collecting the first month's rent, security deposit, and pet damage deposit (within RTA limits) into the brokerage trust account;
- completing the move-in condition inspection (RTB-27) jointly with the tenant;
- handling key handover and move-in;
- providing the Owner a complete placement file: signed agreement, addenda, inspection report, deposit receipts, and tenant contact details.

The Manager does **not** provide ongoing management after handover unless the parties also sign a Property Management Agreement.

## 2. What counts as a "successful placement" (fee is earned when…)

All of the following have happened:

1. a tenant has signed the RTB-1 for the Property;
2. the tenant has paid the first month's rent and deposits (or an approved instalment plan under the RTA — note: instalments are not lawful for deposits unless RTB-approved; **verify current rules**); and
3. the tenant has taken possession (received keys / moved in).

No fee is payable if: the applicant is declined by the Owner without lawful grounds; the applicant never signs or never takes possession; or the Owner withdraws the Property from the market before an applicant is approved (subject to section 7 cost recovery).

## 5. Owner's responsibilities

The Owner will:

- give the Manager true information about the Property, its rental eligibility, strata bylaws and rental restrictions, defects, and pending proceedings;
- keep valid property and liability insurance, and confirm the Property is lawful to rent (including municipal and strata compliance);
- keep the Property vacant, clean, and in move-in condition for viewings and handover;
- provide keys and access on reasonable notice;
- decide on applicants promptly and without discrimination;
- sign documents the Owner is required to sign (e.g., tenancy agreement if the Owner signs as landlord; otherwise authorize the Manager to sign on the Owner's behalf — attach signed authorization in Schedule A);
- collect future rent after handover directly, unless otherwise agreed.

## 6. Manager's responsibilities and limits

The Manager will act with the care, skill, and diligence of a reasonably prudent residential property manager and comply with RESA, the RTA, BCFSA rules, and brokerage policy. The Manager:

- screens applicants in good faith but does **not guarantee** any tenant's future conduct, payment, or vacating;
- is not liable for tenant default, damage, or unlawful acts after placement, or for the accuracy of third-party reports (credit bureau, references) relied on in good faith;
- will hold all trust money received in the brokerage trust account and account to the Owner on placement.

## 7. Termination

- **Before any placement:** either party may end this agreement on written notice (suggested 10 days). The Owner must pay documented out-of-pocket costs already incurred, and, if the Owner terminates after approving an applicant but before signing, reasonable compensation for work completed.
- **After a successful placement:** the agreement ends automatically when the placement file is delivered to the Owner under section 1; sections 3, 6, 8, 9 survive.
- If the Manager cannot place a tenant within the exclusivity period, the agreement lapses unless extended in writing.

## 8. General

- **Liability cap:** the Manager's total liability under this agreement is limited to the placement fee actually paid (negotiable — review with counsel). The Owner indemnifies the Brokerage, Licensee, and managing broker against claims arising from the Property or the placement, except those caused by the Manager's negligence or willful misconduct.
- **Governing law:** British Columbia. Disputes between Owner and Manager: negotiation, then mediation, then arbitration under the BC *Arbitration Act* (Vancouver, single arbitrator). Tenant disputes go to the RTB.
- **Entire agreement:** changes must be in writing and signed by both parties.
- The Owner consents to the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information as required to provide the services, under PIPA.
