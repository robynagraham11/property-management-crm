### ⚠️ Gap alert — stop and fix now

- **Not yet licensed, or licensed category is wrong** → you cannot provide property management services for remuneration. Getting paid or holding yourself out as a property manager while unlicensed risks RESA offence penalties (fines and, in serious cases, prosecution) and orders from BCFSA. **Do not proceed until your licence and brokerage engagement are confirmed.**
- **No business licence** → municipal fines in the City/Township of Langley, and possible enforcement.
- **No trust account approval** → you cannot receive any rent or deposits. This blocks Models B and C entirely.
- **No written service agreement or DORT** → RESA breaches from the first owner meeting onward.

## 2. The three regulators you answer to

1. **BCFSA** — licenses and disciplines you under RESA. They regulate *how* you run the business (licensing, trust money, disclosures, records).
2. **Residential Tenancy Branch (RTB)** — decides disputes between landlords and tenants under the RTA. They regulate *tenancy conduct*: rent, deposits, notices, evictions.
3. **Your municipality** (City of Langley / Township of Langley) — business licensing. Also relevant: strata corporations under the Strata Property Act for strata-titled rentals.

Note: the **Business Practices and Consumer Protection Act** does not govern tenancy agreements — the RTA does. The RTB, not consumer regulators, hears tenancy disputes.

## 3. Scope guardrails (residential only)

- This toolkit covers **residential tenancies** under the RTA only. Commercial tenancies are out of scope — do not reuse these documents for commercial premises.
- **Strata corporations** hire *strata managers* (separate BCFSA licence). Your licence covers managing the *tenancies inside* strata units, not managing the strata corporation itself. Never accept a strata-management retainer on this licence.
- If a client asks about commercial leasing or strata management, refer out or decline.

## 4. Money rules you must never bend

- Security deposit: max **half a month's rent**. Pet damage deposit: max **half a month's rent**. Combined max: **one month's rent**. *(VERIFY: current RTA maximums before every tenancy.)*
- Deposit interest: prescribed by regulation — currently **0%** and has been for years. *(VERIFY: current prescribed rate on the RTB website.)*
- Return deposits within **15 days** of the tenant moving out (RTA s. 21), or file a dispute-resolution application with the RTB.
- Rent increases: once every **12 months**, by **written notice served at least 3 full months before the effective date**, up to the annual provincial cap — 3.0% for 2025 and 3.0% announced for 2026. *(VERIFY: the current-year cap on the RTB website before serving each notice.)*
- All owner and tenant money flows through the **brokerage trust account**; fees are deducted only as your agreement allows and are paid to the **brokerage**, never to you personally (RESA s. 35).

## 5. Assumptions made in this toolkit

- **Assumption:** You hold (or are obtaining) a BCFSA rental property management licence engaged with Stonehaus Realty Corp. If not, stop at the gap alert above.
- **Assumption:** Fees shown in the agreements are market-typical placeholders for Langley — you set your own before use.
- **Assumption:** You may operate in both the City of Langley and Township of Langley — confirm which licences you need.
- **Assumption:** Tenancies are month-to-month or fixed-term residential tenancies under the RTA.

## 6. Before first use — legal review

These documents are AI-drafted and are **not a substitute for review by a BC lawyer**, and do not override your brokerage's own policies, procedures, and approved forms (brokerage policy wins). The three that most warrant lawyer review before first use:

1. **Property Management Agreement** (`01`) — your core revenue contract and RESA s. 30 compliance instrument.
2. **Tenant Placement Service Agreement** (`02`) — fee and guarantee terms are where disputes with owners are born.
3. **Strata Rental Addendum** (`04`) — it must dovetail with actual strata bylaws, which vary building by building.

Also confirm with your **managing broker** that each document complies with brokerage policy before sending it to anyone.
