## B. Statutory RTB forms (use official versions only)

Download current versions at: **gov.bc.ca/landlordtenant → Forms** *(https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/calculators-and-resources/tenancy-forms/forms-listed-number)*

> **Form numbers verified against the RTB "forms listed by number" page (updated September 3, 2026).** Notice numbering changed in recent years — the 10-day unpaid-rent notice is now **RTB-30**, and landlord's-use notices are now **3-month notices generated through the RTB web portal (RTB-32L)**. Re-verify at the link above before each use.

| # | Form | What it is | When required | Who signs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| B1 | **RTB-1 — Residential Tenancy Agreement** | The standard BC tenancy agreement with all mandatory RTA terms built in. | **Every tenancy.** Mandatory form; any extra terms must be in a lawful addendum attached to it. | Owner (or Manager as agent) + all adult tenants. |
| B2 | **RTB-27 — Condition Inspection Report** | Records the unit's condition with photos/notes. | **Move-in AND move-out.** Without the move-in report the landlord loses the right to claim against the security/pet damage deposit (RTA ss. 24, 36 — *verify*). Offer the tenant a chance to participate in both inspections. If the tenant won't schedule the move-out inspection, use **RTB-22 — Notice of Final Opportunity to Schedule a Condition Inspection**. | Owner/Manager and Tenant (tenant signature optional if they don't participate — note on the form). |
| B3 | **RTB-7 — Notice of Rent Increase (Residential Rental Units)** | Official rent increase notice. | Only with **3 full months' written notice**, no more than **once every 12 months**, at or below the **annual percentage cap** set by regulation (*2026 cap: verify at the RTB rent-increase page*). Serve on the official form only. | Owner/Manager. Tenant receives. |
| B4 | **RTB-30 — 10 Day Notice to End Tenancy (Unpaid Rent or Utilities)** | Ends tenancy if rent/utilities unpaid. | When rent or utilities are not paid in full and on time; the tenant has **5 days to pay and cancel** the notice. | Owner/Manager. |
| B5 | **RTB-33 — One Month Notice to End Tenancy** | Combined one-month notice: the **landlord** uses it to end a tenancy **for cause** (repeated late payment, serious interference, unlawful acts, etc.); the **tenant** uses it to give notice to move out. | Landlord: for the specific grounds in RTA s. 47 (*verify*), one full month. Tenant: at least one full month, effective the day before rent is due. | Whichever party gives the notice. |
| B6 | **RTB-32L — Three Month Notice to End Tenancy (Landlord's Use of Property)** | Ends the tenancy so the owner (or a close family member) can occupy, or after a sale where the buyer will occupy. **Generated through the RTB web portal** — not a downloadable form (since July 18, 2024). | Per RTA s. 49; **compensation of one month's rent** is generally owed under RTA s. 51 (*verify current rules*). Cannot be used against a fixed term unless the agreement allows it. | Owner/Manager via the RTB portal (tenancydispute.gov.bc.ca → Landlord Use). |
| B7 | **RTB-29 — Four Month Notice to End Tenancy (Demolition or Conversion)** | Ends tenancy to demolish, extensively renovate, or convert the unit. | Per RTA s. 49.1; compensation rules apply (*verify*). | Owner/Manager. |
| B8 | **RTB-8 — Mutual Agreement to End a Tenancy** | Voluntarily ends a tenancy early by agreement. | When both sides agree to end early — safer than a verbal handshake. | Owner/Manager + Tenant. |
| B9 | **RTB-12L series — Application for Dispute Resolution (Landlord)** | Starts an RTB case: **RTB-12L-CT** (current tenancy), **RTB-12L-PT** (past tenancy), **RTB-12L-DR** (direct request — unpaid rent/utilities), **RTB-12L-EXH** (expedited hearing), **RTB-12L-O** (other). | To enforce rights — deposits, damage claims, unpaid rent, or possession after a notice. | Owner/Manager. |
| B10 | **RTB-46 — Landlord Direct Request Worksheet** | Supporting worksheet for the direct-request application (RTB-12L-DR). | File together with the direct request for unpaid rent/utilities. | Owner/Manager. |
| B11 | **RTB-51 — Address for Service** | Official form to provide the landlord's address for service to the tenant. | At the start of the tenancy (RTA duty — *verify current section*). Our branded C4 notice can be used instead, but RTB-51 is the official option. | Owner/Manager; tenant receives. |
| B12 | **RTB-47 — Tenant Notice of Forwarding Address** | Tenant's notice giving a forwarding address for return of the security deposit. | At or after move-out; the 15-day deposit clock starts when you receive it. | Tenant. |
| B13 | **RTB-24 — Notice Terminating or Restricting a Service or Facility** | Notice if a service or facility (e.g., parking, laundry, storage) is removed or restricted. | Before removing or restricting any service/facility; compensation rules apply (*verify*). | Owner/Manager. |

> **Never reuse old downloaded forms mid-cycle:** the RTB updates forms when the law changes. Re-download from the RTB forms page before each significant step, and keep the file date in your records.

## C. Manager-drafted templates (no official form exists — use these)

These are drafted to comply with the RTA. They are branded templates you complete per file.

### C1 — Notice of Entry (RTA s. 29)

> **The law:** a landlord may enter the unit **8 a.m.–9 p.m.** with **at least 24 hours' written notice** stating the date, time window, and reason, where the reason is lawful (inspections, repairs, showing to prospective tenants/purchasers near the end of a tenancy, or other reasonable purposes). **No notice is needed in an emergency**, or if the tenant gives consent at the time. Post or email this notice; keep a copy.

### C3 — Pet Addendum (attach to RTB-1 where pets are allowed)

> The RTA lets a landlord charge a **pet damage deposit of up to half a month's rent** (in addition to the security deposit of up to half a month's rent). Set the pet rules here so they become additional terms of the RTB-1.

```
PET ADDENDUM to Residential Tenancy Agreement dated ________
Between Owner (through Stonehaus Realty Corp.) and Tenant(s) __________

1. Approved pet(s): ______________________ (type, name, description)
2. The Tenant is responsible for the pet's behaviour and for damage
   caused by the pet (beyond reasonable wear and tear).
3. The Tenant will comply with all strata bylaws, municipal animal
   control laws, and leash/licence rules that apply.
4. The Tenant will not bring additional or substitute pets without
   the Owner's written approval.
5. If the Tenant breaches this addendum, the Owner may serve notice
   under the RTA as for a material term breach.

Tenant: __________________ Date: ______  Owner/Agent: ______________ Date: ______
```

## D. Security deposit, pet deposit, and interest — the non-negotiable rules

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Maximums | Security deposit: **½ month's rent**. Pet damage deposit: **½ month's rent** (pets only). Nothing else may be charged or held (no "key deposits", no "cleaning deposits", no application fees — RTA s. 15 *(verify)*). |
| Interest | Interest accrues at the rate prescribed each year under the RTA Regulation and must be credited to the tenant **annually** and on return of the deposit. *Verify the current year's rate at the RTB website.* |
| Return | Return the deposit within **15 days** of the tenant giving a forwarding address in writing — unless the tenant agrees in writing to a deduction, or the RTB orders otherwise. |
| Deductions | Only for damage beyond reasonable wear and tear, unpaid rent/utilities, and lawful costs — supported by the RTB-27 reports. A tenant may dispute via the RTB; a wrongful retention can cost **double** (RTA s. 38 *(verify)*). |
| Holding in trust | While the brokerage holds deposits, they are trust monies in the brokerage trust account — never touched except as the RTA allows. |

## E. Rent increases — quick reference

- Use **RTB-7 only**, with the correct amount.
- **3 full months'** written notice; effective date **at least 12 months** after the tenancy started or the last increase.
- Amount cannot exceed the **annual cap set by the Province** each year. *Verify the current-year cap and any landlord-use exemptions at the RTB rent-increase page before serving any notice.*

## F. Strata properties — required steps for every strata tenancy

| Step | Document | When | Who signs |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Confirm the strata allows rentals and any conditions (rental caps, age, minimum terms). | **Form B — Information Certificate** (request from the strata) + bylaws/rules. | Before listing/marketing. | Strata issues Form B. |
| 2. Give the tenant the bylaws and rules that apply. | Copy of **current bylaws and rules**. | Before the RTB-1 is signed (RTA strata provisions — *verify section*). | Tenant acknowledges receipt. |
| 3. Notice of tenant's responsibilities. | **Form K — Notice of Tenant's Responsibilities** (Strata Property Act, prescribed form). | At the start of the tenancy; provide to the strata as the bylaws require. | **Tenant signs; landlord/agent provides.** |
| 4. Tenant breaches a bylaw | Correspondence + RTA notice (usually RTB-33 — one month for cause, material breach). | If the strata issues a complaint or fine about tenant conduct. | Owner/Manager. |

> The owner remains responsible to the strata for **fines**; recovering them from the tenant happens through the RTA (usually as a claim in an RTB dispute or a deduction from the deposit with agreement). Keep every bylaw complaint in writing.

## G. One-page compliance checklist for each tenancy (photocopy per file)

**Before signing (marketing phase):**
- [ ] BCFSA disclosure (A2) given to each applicant before services begin
- [ ] Strata Form B + bylaws reviewed (strata units only)
- [ ] Municipal rental business licence confirmed (City or Township of Langley)
- [ ] Advertising complies with strata rules and RESA rules (brokerage name visible)

**Signing:**
- [ ] RTB-1 + any lawful addenda (pet addendum C3 if applicable)
- [ ] Notice of owner's name and address for service (C4 template, or official form RTB-51)
- [ ] Form K signed by tenant (strata only)
- [ ] Bylaws/rules copy given to tenant (strata only)

**Move-in:**
- [ ] RTB-27 move-in inspection (offer tenant participation; give copy)
- [ ] First month's rent + security deposit (max ½ month) + pet deposit (max ½ month) → brokerage trust account
- [ ] Rent receipt issued

**During tenancy:**
- [ ] Interest on deposits credited annually (verify current rate)
- [ ] Notices served only on current official forms, with required lead times (RTB-7, RTB-30, RTB-33, RTB-32L via portal, RTB-29, RTB-24)
- [ ] Entry only under RTA s. 29 rules (C1)

**Move-out:**
- [ ] RTB-27 move-out inspection (offer tenant participation; use RTB-22 if the tenant won't schedule it)
- [ ] Forwarding address requested in writing (tenant may use RTB-47)
- [ ] Deposit returned within 15 days of forwarding address, or agreement/RTB order for deductions
