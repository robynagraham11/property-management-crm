> **FOR RENTAL PROPERTY OWNERS** — I find, screen and place a great tenant in your rental. Once the lease is signed and the keys are handed over, you manage the tenancy yourself. It's done right and it's done by the book.

## What I handle

- Rental market analysis and pricing advice
- Pro photos, listing copy and online marketing
- Fielding every enquiry and running the showings
- Rental applications, credit checks and references (with the applicant's written consent)
- A shortlist and my recommendation. **You make the final call**
- The BC Residential Tenancy Agreement (RTB-1), prepared and signed
- Collecting the first month's rent and the deposits
- Move-in Condition Inspection Report (RTB-27) [if included]

## What stays with you after move-in

- Collecting rent each month
- Repairs, maintenance and tenant calls
- Rent increases, notices and renewals
- The move-out inspection and deposit return

## The process

1. **Chat** — we go over your property, your goals and your timeline.
2. **Intake form** — you send me the property details and your rules (pets, smoking, term).
3. **Paperwork** — we sign the service agreement and the required BCFSA disclosures.
4. **Prep & price** — we set the rent, book photos and get the unit show-ready.
5. **Market** — the listing goes live. I take every enquiry and run the showings.
6. **Screen** — I check applications, credit, income and landlord references.
7. **You approve** — I give you my top pick and the reasons. You decide.
8. **Sign & keys** — lease signed, deposits collected, inspection done, keys handed over.

## What you'll sign

| Document | What it's for |
|---|---|
| Tenant Placement Services Agreement | Our contract with Stonehaus Realty. It covers what I do, my fee and the term. |
| Payments & Benefits Disclosure (BCFSA) | Lists any payments I'd get from anyone other than you, like referral fees. Usually: none. |
| Residential Tenancy Agreement (RTB-1) | The BC government's standard lease, between you and your new tenant. |
| Condition Inspection Report (RTB-27) | Records the unit's condition at move-in. You need it if you ever want to keep part of the damage deposit. |

## The fee
