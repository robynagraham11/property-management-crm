## 1. Definitions

- **"Tenant"** means a person who enters a tenancy agreement with the Owner (through the Manager) for all or part of the Property.
- **"Rent"** means money payable by a Tenant for the right to possess the rental unit.
- **"Trust monies"** means all money received by the Manager for or on account of the Owner or a Tenant, including rent and security and pet damage deposits.
- **"RTA"** means the *Residential Tenancy Act*, S.B.C. 2002, c. 78, and its regulations, as amended.

## 2. Appointment and scope

2.1 The Owner appoints the Manager as the Owner's exclusive agent to provide the services selected in **Schedule A** for the Property, for the Term.

2.2 The Manager accepts the appointment and agrees to act in accordance with RESA, the RTA, and the BCFSA Real Estate Rules, and to use reasonable care, skill, and diligence.

2.3 **Residential only.** The Manager is authorized to enter into **residential tenancy agreements only**. Commercial, business, storage-only, or non-residential uses are outside this agreement and require separate arrangements.

2.4 The Manager may not bind the Owner to any agreement or expenditure beyond the authority in this agreement (see clause 8).

## 3. Term

3.1 The term begins on the Effective Date and continues **month to month** until ended under clause 14 (the **"Term"**). *(If a fixed end date is ever preferred, add it here in writing and both parties initial: ______________________)*

## 4. Manager's services

The Manager will provide the services selected in **Schedule A** and, where applicable:

- (a) comply with the RTA when advertising, screening, entering into tenancies, giving notices, and handling deposits;
- (b) keep the Owner reasonably informed, respond to Owner inquiries within **2 business days**, and follow the Owner's lawful instructions;
- (c) hold all trust monies in the Brokerage's trust account and account to the Owner monthly (see clause 9);
- (d) keep records required under RESA and provide copies to the Owner on request;
- (e) never charge any fee to a Tenant except as permitted by the RTA (application fees, move-in/move-out fees, and similar charges to tenants are prohibited).

## 5. Owner's obligations

The Owner will:

- (a) provide accurate information about the Property and disclose anything that could materially affect a tenancy (defects, restrictions, strata rules, rental licensing status);
- (b) provide keys, garage remotes, manuals, and contact information;
- (c) maintain the Property in a state that complies with the RTA (the Owner remains legally responsible for repairs and maintenance under the RTA unless the Manager is expressly authorized and funded to arrange them);
- (d) maintain adequate **property and liability insurance** for the Property and provide proof to the Manager on request (see clause 12);
- (e) give the Manager written authority to act before any third party requires it (e.g., strata corporations, utilities, municipalities);
- (f) keep the Property's **municipal residential rental business licence** (if required in the City or Township of Langley) current and provide the licence number: ______________;
- (g) pay the fees and reimburse disbursements under Schedule A and clause 7;
- (h) indemnify the Manager as set out in clause 12.

## 6. Tenancies, deposits, and RTA compliance

6.1 All tenancy agreements will be in the current **RTB-1 Residential Tenancy Agreement** form plus any approved addendum. The Manager signs as the Owner's agent.

6.2 The Owner authorizes the Manager to collect, on the Owner's behalf:

- (a) a security deposit of up to **one half of one month's rent**;
- (b) a pet damage deposit of up to **one half of one month's rent** where pets are permitted.

All deposits are trust monies (clause 9).

6.3 The Owner acknowledges that:

- (a) security deposit **interest** accrues at the rate prescribed under the RTA each year and must be credited to the Tenant annually, and on return of the deposit;
- (b) deposits may only be retained with the Tenant's written agreement or an RTB order;
- (c) an unlawful deduction or late return can expose the Owner to an RTB order for **double the deposit** (RTA s. 38). *(Verify the current provision and prescribed interest rate.)*

6.4 The Owner authorizes the Manager to serve notices, participate in move-in and move-out condition inspections (RTB-27), and represent the Owner in RTB proceedings arising from a tenancy at the Property. *(RTB proceedings outside the services in Schedule A are billed at **$95/hour** + GST — assumed, adjust if agreed otherwise.)*

## 7. Fees and disbursements

7.1 Fees are as set out in **Schedule A**. All fees are payable to the **Brokerage**, plus **GST (currently 5%)**.

7.2 **Disbursements** (advertising, credit checks, locksmith, courier, etc.) are reimbursed at cost with receipts, or paid directly by the Owner where arranged.

7.3 The Manager may deduct earned fees and authorized disbursements from monies collected for the Owner, with a statement. If collected monies are insufficient, the Owner will pay the invoiced balance within **15 days**.

7.4 If the Owner cancels an advertising campaign, the Owner remains responsible for costs already incurred.

## 8. Authority limits

8.1 The Manager may arrange routine maintenance and repairs up to **$500 per occurrence** *(assumed — change here if you agree a different number: ____)* without prior approval. Anything above that (and any emergency exceeding it) requires the Owner's approval, which the Owner will not unreasonably withhold or delay. Emergency safety work needed to keep the unit habitable may proceed without approval, and the Owner will reimburse it.

8.2 The Manager may not borrow, mortgage, or encumber the Property, or execute documents creating a lien or legal claim against the Owner.

## 9. Trust monies and accounting

9.1 The Manager will deposit all trust monies into the Brokerage's **BCFSA-authorized trust account** and will not co-mingle them with other funds.

9.2 Security and pet damage deposits are held in trust for the Tenants, remain the Tenants' property, and are dealt with strictly under the RTA.

9.3 The Manager will provide the Owner a **monthly statement** showing rents collected, deposits held, disbursements, fees, and the net amount paid to the Owner.

9.4 Net monies due to the Owner are paid monthly (or per Schedule A) by the Brokerage. *(Verify any brokerage-specific trust account and interest arrangements with the managing broker.)*

## 10. Owner's representations

The Owner represents and warrants that:

- (a) the Owner has the right to rent the Property and to enter this agreement;
- (b) the Property and any suite comply with applicable laws (including any municipal secondary-suite requirements and rental licensing); and
- (c) if the Property is a **strata lot**, the Owner has provided current strata bylaws and rules and any rental restriction information to the Manager, and holds all required strata permissions.

## 11. Strata properties (where applicable)

11.1 Where the Property is a strata lot:

- (a) the Manager will confirm the strata's **rental bylaws** (occupancy restrictions, rental caps, pet bylaws, move-in/move-out procedures, common property rules) before marketing;
- (b) the Owner remains responsible for complying with the *Strata Property Act*, the bylaws, and the rules, and for any strata fines or fees charged to the Owner's strata lot;
- (c) the Manager will ensure each Tenant receives **Form K — Notice of Tenant's Responsibilities** (and a copy of applicable bylaws and rules) at the start of the tenancy;
- (d) Tenant breaches of bylaws are handled under the RTA and the Strata Property Act; the Owner authorizes the Manager to respond to strata correspondence about tenancy matters.

## 12. Insurance, liability, and indemnity

12.1 **Owner's insurance.** The Owner must maintain property and liability insurance on the Property and provide proof on request. The Owner acknowledges the Manager is not responsible for casualty losses, rent loss, or damage to the Property, except as caused by the Manager's gross negligence or willful misconduct.

12.2 **Manager's liability.** The Manager is liable only for direct loss caused by the Manager's **gross negligence or willful misconduct**, and that liability is capped at **the total fees paid under this agreement in the preceding 3 months**. *(Assumed — review with counsel if you want a different cap.)*

12.3 **No liability for tenants.** The Manager is not liable for: Tenant conduct; unpaid rent; damage by Tenants or third parties; environmental conditions; or the financial condition of any Tenant. The Manager's screening services are advisory — the Owner makes the final decision to accept or reject an applicant.

12.4 **Indemnity.** The Owner indemnifies and holds harmless the Manager and the Brokerage from all claims, costs, and liabilities arising from the ownership or condition of the Property or any tenancy, except to the extent caused by the Manager's gross negligence or willful misconduct.

## 13. Dispute resolution and governing law

13.1 The parties will first try to resolve disputes by good-faith negotiation. If not resolved within **30 days**, either party may refer the dispute to mediation with a mutually agreed mediator (costs shared equally) before starting court proceedings.

13.2 **Owner–Manager disputes** are not RTB tenancy disputes; they are governed by this agreement and are subject to the **exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of British Columbia** (or the Civil Resolution Tribunal where a claim is within its monetary jurisdiction — *verify current CRT limits*). Tenancy disputes between the Owner and a Tenant remain with the RTB.

13.3 This agreement is governed by the laws of **British Columbia**, including RESA and the RTA.

## 14. Termination

14.1 Either party may end this agreement on **30 days' written notice** (delivered per clause 17), effective at the end of a month.

14.2 The Owner may end it immediately on written notice for: suspension or cancellation of the Brokerage's licence; material breach by the Manager not cured within 10 days of written notice; or insolvency of the Brokerage.

14.3 The Manager may end it immediately on written notice for: the Owner's failure to pay fees or fund repairs after 10 days' written notice; the Owner's material breach (including unlicensed or unlawful rental of the Property); or the Owner's instruction to do something unlawful.

14.4 On termination, the Manager will promptly hand over records, keys, and trust monies in accordance with law and any brokerage requirements. Fees earned to the date of termination, and disbursements incurred, remain payable. Tenant deposits remain in trust until dealt with under the RTA.

14.5 If the Property is **sold**, this agreement ends on completion of the sale, and the Manager will cooperate in a transition. If the Manager is asked to assist with the new owner's tenancy, a new agreement will be signed.

## 15. Assignment

15.1 The Owner may not assign this agreement without the Manager's written consent. If a licensee leaves the Brokerage, the Brokerage may (with the Owner's consent, not to be unreasonably withheld) assign this agreement to another licensee of the Brokerage; otherwise either party may end it on 30 days' notice.

## 16. General

16.1 **Entire agreement:** This agreement (with its Schedules) is the whole agreement between the parties about managing the Property, replacing earlier discussions.

16.2 **Severability:** If any provision is unenforceable, the rest remains in force, and the provision is read down only as far as necessary.

16.3 **Amendment:** Changes must be in writing and signed by both parties.

16.4 **Independent contractor:** The Manager is an independent contractor, not an employee of the Owner.

## 17. Notices

17.1 Notices under this agreement are delivered personally, by email (to the addresses above), or by prepaid mail. Email is effective the day it is sent (if no bounce notice is received); mail is deemed received **5 days** after mailing.
