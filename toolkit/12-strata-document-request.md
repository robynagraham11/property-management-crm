> **HOW TO USE:** Send this to the strata corporation (or its property manager) when onboarding a strata rental unit, **before advertising**. You need Form B to confirm rental bylaws and restrictions. The strata may charge a fee for Form B — confirm the amount and who pays it (usually the owner). Keep the response in the file.

Dear [contact],

We manage the rental of the above strata lot on behalf of the owner, [owner name]. To comply with the *Strata Property Act* and the *Residential Tenancy Act* before marketing the unit for rent, please provide:

1. **Form B — Information Certificate** for the strata lot, showing any rental restrictions, unpaid fines, and current fees;
2. A copy of the **current bylaws and rules**;
3. Confirmation of any **rental-specific rules** — occupancy or rental caps, minimum or maximum tenancy terms, age restrictions, and any rental application or fee requirements;
4. The **move-in/move-out rules** — booking procedures, elevator use, move-in fees;
5. **Pet bylaws**, if any; and
6. The correct contact for submitting **Form K — Notice of Tenant's Responsibilities**.

The owner has authorized us to request these records and to act on their behalf for tenancy matters.
