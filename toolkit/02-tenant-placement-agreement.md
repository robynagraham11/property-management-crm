## 3. Services included

The Brokerage, through the Licensee, will:

- recommend a rental rate based on current market comparables,
- advertise the property, including photos and listing copy,
- respond to enquiries and conduct showings,
- collect rental applications and screening consents,
- complete reference, employment, income and credit checks that the applicant has consented to,
- present screened applicants to the Landlord with a written summary,
- prepare the residential tenancy agreement on the Landlord's instructions, and
- **[optional: conduct the move-in condition inspection with the tenant and prepare the Condition Inspection Report]**.

## 4. Services not included

After the placement is complete, the Landlord is responsible for everything else. That includes rent collection, deposits and their return, maintenance and repairs, tenant communications, rent increases, renewals, notices, inspections, dispute resolution and ending the tenancy.

If the Landlord wants ongoing management, the parties will sign a separate property management agreement.

## 5. Authority

- The Landlord makes the final decision on which applicant to accept. The Brokerage does not approve or decline applicants on the Landlord's behalf.
- Tenancy agreement signing: ☐ [the Landlord signs the tenancy agreement personally] or ☐ [the Landlord authorizes the Brokerage to sign the tenancy agreement as agent, on written approval of the tenant and terms].
- The Brokerage has no authority to arrange repairs, spend money or enter into contracts for the Landlord, other than the advertising in section 3.

- **Fee:** **50% of one month's rent, minimum $1,000**, plus GST. *(Assumed per the Rent with Robyn kit — adjust: [________])*
- The fee is earned when a tenant approved by the Landlord signs a tenancy agreement.

- **Advertising costs:** ☒ [charged at cost, to a maximum of **$500**, with the Landlord's prior approval] or ☐ [included in the fee].
- If the Landlord rents to an applicant introduced by the Brokerage within **[90]** days after this agreement ends, the fee is still payable.
- **[Optional replacement term:** if the tenant ends the tenancy within **[90]** days of the start date for reasons unrelated to the Landlord's conduct, the Brokerage will place one replacement tenant for advertising costs only.**]**

## 7. Deposits and money — choose one

- ☐ **Option A:** The tenant pays the security deposit, any pet damage deposit and first month's rent **directly to the Landlord**. The Brokerage holds no money for this tenancy.
- ☐ **Option B:** The Brokerage collects the security deposit, any pet damage deposit and first month's rent **into its trust account**. It will pay those amounts to the Landlord, less the placement fee and approved costs, within **[7]** business days of the tenancy start date, with a written statement.

Under either option, the Landlord holds the deposits for the rest of the tenancy and is responsible for returning them under the Residential Tenancy Act. Each deposit is limited to half of one month's rent.

## 8. Landlord's commitments

The Landlord confirms that:

- every registered owner has signed this agreement, or the signer has written authority to sign for the others,
- the property can be lawfully rented, including under strata bylaws and municipal rules,
- the information in the Property Information and Authorization form is accurate and complete,
- they will disclose known defects, hazards and anything a tenant would reasonably want to know,
- the property will be clean, safe and ready for occupancy on the tenancy start date,
- they will give selection instructions that comply with the **BC Human Rights Code**, and
- they hold appropriate insurance for a rented property.

## 9. Completion

The placement is complete when the tenancy agreement is signed, the items in section 7 are settled, and the Brokerage delivers the **Placement Completion and Handover Checklist** with the tenancy file. From that point the Brokerage provides no further services for the property.

## 10. Ending this agreement early

- Either party may end this agreement with **[7]** days' written notice.
- If the Landlord ends it before a tenant is placed, the Landlord pays ☒ [advertising costs incurred] or ☐ [a cancellation fee of $________ plus GST].
- The Brokerage may end it immediately if the Landlord gives instructions that are unlawful or that would breach the Brokerage's professional obligations.

## 11. Privacy and records

- The Brokerage collects, uses and discloses personal information only to provide these services and to meet its legal obligations. Applicant information is shared with the Landlord only as needed to choose a tenant.
- The Brokerage keeps records of this placement for the period the Real Estate Services Rules require.
- The Landlord will keep applicant and tenant information confidential and use it only for the tenancy.

## 12. General

- The Brokerage does not guarantee a tenant's conduct, payment of rent or care of the property.
- The Licensee is not a lawyer and does not give legal or tax advice.
- Non-resident landlords are responsible for their own tax obligations on rental income.
- This is the whole agreement for these services. Changes must be in writing and signed by both parties.
- British Columbia law governs this agreement.
