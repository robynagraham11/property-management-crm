## Landlord's responsibilities from here

- Collecting rent and holding the deposits, including returning them with any interest owed under the Residential Tenancy Act.
- If the landlord is doing the move-in inspection, completing it with the tenant and giving them the signed report within 7 days.
- Repairs, maintenance and emergency calls.
- Rent increases, notices and any dispute resolution, using Residential Tenancy Branch forms and timelines.
- Keeping the tenancy records and protecting the tenant's personal information.
