## 2. What documents the competitors actually use

### RentalPropertyManagers.ca (full published set — 9 documents)

1. **Disclosure of Representation in Trading Services** — landlord version (BCFSA/RECBC prescribed)
2. **Disclosure for Residential Tenancies** — tenant version (BCFSA/RECBC prescribed)
3. **RTB-1** — Residential Tenancy Agreement
4. **Rental Application Form for Tenants** — their own branded form *(we reference this but haven't drafted it — **GAP**)*
5. **Privacy and Consent Statement** — PIPA-compliant consent for collecting personal information *(not drafted — **GAP**)*
6. **RTB-27** — Condition Inspection Report
7. **Pre-Authorized Debit (PAD) Agreement** — for automated rent collection *(not drafted — **GAP**)*
8. **Monthly Statement to Landlord (sample)** — itemized owner accounting *(referenced in our agreement clause 9.3 but not drafted — **GAP**)*
9. **RTB-7** — Notice of Rent Increase

### Dwell Property Management (public owner forms)

- **Pre-Authorized Debit (PAD) Agreement** (standard + special-levy variants)
- **Strata document request forms** — for Form B (Information Certificate) and Form F *(nice-to-have — **GAP**)*
- **Form K** — Notice of Tenant's Responsibilities

### APM — practices (not just documents)

- Management fee benchmark: "most management will cost **10%**"
- Defends placement fees as covering advertising + screening costs (states hard costs alone can exceed $200/vacancy)
- **Annual inspections at minimum**; recommends frequent inspections (insurance clauses often require inspection intervals)
- Monthly **itemized owner statements** with copies of invoices
- Owner and tenant resource pages + FAQ

### HomeLife Property Rentals (Langley competitor)

- Explicit **two-tier model**: Tenant Placement Service vs Full Property Management — matches our Model 3 and Model 2
- Tenant applications run through **software (Rhenti)** with tenant pre-approval
- Owner portal login

## 4. Important corrections found while researching (applied to the toolkit)

The RTB renumbered its notice forms. The toolkit has been corrected to the **official list of September 3, 2026**:

- **RTB-30** = 10 Day Notice (unpaid rent/utilities) — *(previously mislabelled as RTB-29)*
- **RTB-33** = One Month Notice to End Tenancy — combined landlord-cause and tenant notice — *(previously split across RTB-30/RTB-33)*
- **RTB-32L** = Three Month Notice (landlord's use) — generated **through the RTB web portal**, not a downloadable form (since July 18, 2024) — *(previously "RTB-31 two-month")*
- **RTB-29** = Four Month Notice (demolition/conversion) — *(previously "RTB-32")*
- **RTB-8** = Mutual Agreement to End a Tenancy
- Also added to the statutory list: **RTB-22** (final opportunity for move-out inspection), **RTB-24** (restricting a service/facility), **RTB-46** (direct request worksheet), **RTB-47** (tenant forwarding address), **RTB-51** (address for service)
