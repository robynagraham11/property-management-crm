## The placement flow (what the landlord sees)

1. Discovery call and verbal agreement with the landlord.
2. Landlord completes **Document 2, Property Information and Authorization** (file `05`).
3. Review and sign the **BCFSA Disclosure of Representation in Trading Services** with the landlord, before any leasing services start.
4. Landlord and brokerage sign **Document 1, Tenant Placement Service Agreement** (file `02`).
5. Advertise and show the property. Every applicant completes **Documents 3 and 4** (files `06` and `07`).
6. Screen applicants, then present **Document 5** (file `08`) to the landlord. The landlord chooses.
7. Prepare the tenancy agreement (RTB-1 or brokerage equivalent), collect deposits as the agreement directs, and complete the move-in inspection (RTB-27) if it is in scope.
8. Sign off **Document 6** (file `09`) with the landlord. Your services end there.

## 1 · Owner onboarding — before any marketing

- [ ] Verbal agreement on scope and fee
- [ ] Owner intake form received (`05-property-information-and-authorization`)
- [ ] Ownership confirmed (title search), strata bylaws or rental restrictions checked, owner's insurance noted
- [ ] Service agreement signed by the owner and Stonehaus (`02-tenant-placement-agreement`)
- [ ] Scope, fee, term, cancellation, who holds the deposits confirmed
- [ ] **Payments & Benefits Disclosure** given with the info page — write "None anticipated" if there are none *(BCFSA s. 56/58 — verify)*
- [ ] **Interest in Trade** check: am I or an associate the tenant or the landlord? If yes, give the disclosure before any agreement — Part B if I or an associate is renting it; Part C if I or an associate owns it *(BCFSA s. 53 — verify)*
- [ ] File opened with Stonehaus and agreement submitted to the managing broker

## 2 · Prep & marketing

- [ ] Rental CMA done and rent set with the owner
- [ ] Photos, listing copy and showing schedule set up
- [ ] Pets, smoking, utilities, parking, term all spelled out
- [ ] Listing live
- [ ] Advertising follows BCFSA rules: brokerage name shown, no discriminatory wording (BC Human Rights Code)

## 3 · Showings & applications

- [ ] **Tenant disclosure** given to each unrepresented prospect before providing services — two pages, signed by me; ask them to initial; keep a copy *(BCFSA s. 54/55 — verify)*
- [ ] Applications received with written consent for credit and reference checks (`06` + `07`)
- [ ] Personal info handled under BC PIPA
- [ ] Screening done: ID, credit, income, employment, landlord references
- [ ] Shortlist and recommendation sent to the owner. Owner's approval in writing (`08`)
- [ ] Declined applicants notified

## 4 · Lease & move-in

- [ ] RTB-1 prepared and signed by the landlord and every adult tenant
- [ ] Addendum terms follow the RTA. Copy to the tenant within 21 days
- [ ] Security deposit (max ½ month) and pet damage deposit (max ½ month) collected
- [ ] Held in Stonehaus trust or passed to the owner, as set out in the agreement
- [ ] First month's rent collected; proof of tenant insurance received [if required]
- [ ] Move-in inspection done with the tenant and photos taken
- [ ] Tenant gets a copy within 7 days (RTB-27)
- [ ] Keys handed over and receipt signed

## 5 · Close-out

- [ ] Owner hand-off package: signed RTB-1, RTB-27 and photos, tenant contacts, deposit details (`09`)
- [ ] Fee invoiced and paid; funds moved through the brokerage
- [ ] Full file submitted to Stonehaus and closed. Offer full management if it's a fit
